Roofing FAQs

Common Questions from Homeowners

Helpful answers before you schedule your inspection.

How do I know whether I need a roof repair or full replacement?

The answer depends on roof age, the size and location of the damage, shingle condition, flashing, decking, and whether leaks are isolated or widespread.

How long does a typical roof replacement take?

Many residential shingle roofs can be completed in one to several working days, depending on size, complexity, weather, decking repairs, and site access.

Do you replace damaged plywood?

Yes. Once the existing roof is removed, damaged or deteriorated decking can be identified and replaced as needed before the new system is installed.

Do you provide roof inspections and estimates?

Yes. Blueprint Exteriors provides roofing evaluations and written estimates for roof replacement, repair, storm damage, and related exterior work.

Can you complete gutters, fascia, soffits, or chimney flashing with the roof?

Yes. Coordinating related roofline work can create a cleaner installation and reduce the need for multiple contractors.
